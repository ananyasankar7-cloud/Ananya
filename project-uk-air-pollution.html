UK Air Pollution Trends & Government Intervention

My MSc dissertation (Distinction): did government action actually change UK air quality over 50 years?

50 yearsof UK data
ARIMAtime-series forecasting
DistinctionUniversity of Leicester

The question

I wanted to know whether government interventions on air pollution left a measurable mark on the data, or whether the trends would have happened anyway.

What I did

I analysed 50 years of UK air pollution data with statistical methods and ARIMA time-series modelling and forecasting to assess the effect of government intervention. I then built interactive dashboards in R Shiny and ArcGIS so the results could be explored by region and over time.
